Enterprise IT services: common questions

Does SLSG support projects outside Pune?

SLSG is based in Pune, with offices in Kharadi and Katraj, and offers pan-India service delivery. Share your project locations so the team can discuss deployment and support arrangements.

What should I prepare before requesting a proposal?

Share your business objective, existing systems, number of users or devices, site locations, timeline, and budget range. For maintenance enquiries, include an equipment inventory and your current support arrangements.

Can I discuss support for existing infrastructure?

Yes. The managed services portfolio includes AMC support, preventive maintenance, infrastructure reviews, helpdesk setup, and network integration. Coverage depends on the agreed equipment, locations, and scope.
