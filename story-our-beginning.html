The SLSG story: experience behind enterprise technology.

How experience in defence and enterprise leadership shaped a Pune-based technology company focused on business value, reliable execution, and long-term relationships.

← All stories

SLSG Infosystems · 4 min read

A different starting point

SLSG Infosystems was founded by professionals with decades of leadership experience in defence and enterprise business environments. The ambition was to create a technology partner grounded in practical experience, sound judgement, and dependable execution.

From products to purpose

Technology decisions begin with a business need. A new platform, device, or service only makes sense when it helps an organization solve a real problem. That is why the company’s philosophy places business value before a product recommendation.

Discipline in delivery

A recommendation is only the beginning. Implementation, support, and accountability matter just as much. Operational discipline means making responsibilities clear and following through on the work that brings an idea into everyday use.

Partnership is a human practice

As AI and automation become part of more business workflows, experience and judgement remain essential. SLSG’s story is built around trust, transparency, and relationships that continue beyond a single purchase.

Meet the people behind SLSG

Founder and CEO Ashok Srivastava brings sales and business development experience across enterprise sectors. COO Anil Kumar brings experience in the Indian Air Force and corporate operations. Their perspectives connect relationship building with service delivery and execution.

Read the leadership profiles and messages to learn more about the people guiding the company.

From Pune to enterprise projects across India

With a head office in Kharadi and a branch in Katraj, SLSG supports enterprise technology requirements through its service portfolio and technology alliances. The focus spans infrastructure, cloud, security, managed IT, workforce solutions, and sustainability.
